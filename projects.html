Dead Skies

A side-scrolling plane shooter built in Godot. Survive four waves of alien machines, shrink down to dodge their fire, burn them with a laser, and charge your super meter to turn your plane into a torpedo. Pick up upgrades between waves, and if you go down your pilot floats away as a ghost. Disclaimer: This project is a prototype. Bug fixes coming soon.
